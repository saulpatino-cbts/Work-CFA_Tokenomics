# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Everyone participating in this repository, its issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and commits must:

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept review of the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion focused on the problem and supporting evidence.
- Respect recorded decisions; raise disagreement through a new issue or decision record rather than ignoring them.
- Protect customer privacy; never publish credentials, private endpoints, customer exports, prompts, or identifying information.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer identified in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) through an established private channel if one is available. If none is available, open a minimal issue requesting a private contact route without naming affected people or including sensitive details. A private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been verified; maintainers must establish one and explain its handling before broader community participation.

For software vulnerabilities, follow the separate [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md). Do not assume a vulnerability-reporting mechanism is the appropriate channel for a conduct complaint.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock violating content and restrict participation. Share reports only as needed for investigation and enforcement; do not promise absolute confidentiality. Describe any conflict of interest and request an alternative contact when the named maintainer is involved.
